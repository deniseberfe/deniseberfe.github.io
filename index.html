How I work

Good operations
is a form of care.

For the customer, for the team, and for the person trying to understand what happens next.

01

Find the real problem

I ask the extra question, trace the friction, and separate symptoms from the thing that actually needs fixing.

02

Make the work legible

I turn scattered context into a shared plan: owners, milestones, definitions, documentation, and next steps.

03

Build the useful version

I care less about impressive complexity and more about systems people will actually use when the week gets busy.

04

Close the loop

I follow the signal after launch, notice what is not working, and make the next iteration better.
